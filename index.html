About the Portal

Skill Bridge is a three-way collaboration ecosystem designed to close the industry-readiness gap in technical education.

🏭 Industry

Companies post live problem statements and internship opportunities, and directly access a pipeline of AICTE-verified, skill-mapped student talent.

🎓 Colleges (AICTE Approved)

Institutions verify their AICTE approval status, list faculty mentors, and track student participation in industry-aligned projects.

🧑‍💻 Students

Students get an AI-generated skill profile, personalized project recommendations, and a chatbot guide for the entire journey.

For Industry Partners

  • Post live/real-world problem statements directly to relevant departments.
  • Filter students by AI-mapped skill profile, not just resumes.
  • Track project progress with participating colleges in real time.
  • Verified access — only AICTE-approved institutions and enrolled students.

For AICTE-Approved Institutions

  • Register using your AICTE Permanent ID for instant verification.
  • Assign faculty coordinators to industry-linked live projects.
  • Monitor student participation and outcomes on a college dashboard.
  • Showcase institutional achievements and industry collaborations.

For Students

  • Get an AI-generated skill profile from your coursework and interests.
  • Discover live industry projects matched to your skill profile.
  • Chat with the AI assistant for guidance on applications and skills to build.
  • Build a verifiable project portfolio recognized by industry partners.
